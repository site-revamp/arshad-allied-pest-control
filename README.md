# Allied Pest Control Homepage Redesign

## 1. Website Overview

Allied Pest Control Services Co. is a Dubai-based pest management company providing residential and commercial pest control, termite protection, bird proofing, and preventive pest management services.

The redesigned homepage focuses on communicating Allied's value proposition more clearly, presenting credible business information, and helping visitors find relevant pest control services quickly. The design targets homeowners, tenants, property managers, restaurants, offices, and commercial property operators across Dubai.

## 2. Main Improvements

### 1. Stronger Homepage Messaging and Value Proposition
- Improved the homepage headline to communicate Allied's pest management services and customer benefits more directly.
- Made key services and Dubai service coverage more prominent.
- Strengthened the visibility of primary contact and quotation actions.
- Addressed the previous lack of immediate clarity about the company's offering and value.

### 2. Stronger Trust Signals and Social Proof
- Improved the presentation of company credentials, service experience, and published guarantees.
- Created clearer opportunities to communicate relevant certifications and customer reviews.
- Prioritised genuine service photography to support the company's professional positioning.
- Addressed the limited visibility of supporting evidence behind the website's credibility claims.

### 3. Improved Visual Hierarchy and Conversion Flow
- Organised service sections, headings, images, and calls-to-action into a clearer structure.
- Made it easier for visitors to identify their pest problems and navigate to relevant treatments.
- Improved the visibility of quotation and contact options.
- Reduced unnecessary navigation and scrolling between service discovery and enquiry actions.

## 3. Design Improvements

- **Visual presentation:** Improved the prominence of service information, credibility elements, and customer-facing messaging.
- **Layout:** Established a clearer hierarchy between the homepage introduction, services, trust information, and conversion sections.
- **User experience:** Simplified the journey from identifying a pest problem to finding a relevant service and requesting assistance.
- **Consistency:** Improved the organisation and presentation of headings, images, service information, and calls-to-action.
- **Overall appearance:** Created a more structured and professional homepage focused on customer confidence, service discovery, and enquiries.

## Project Objective

The redesign addresses the three critical issues identified during the original website research: weak homepage messaging, insufficient trust signals, and inconsistent visual hierarchy and conversion flow. The overall objective is to communicate Allied Pest Control's value more effectively, strengthen customer confidence, and make requesting pest management services easier.
